TESS-LUMEN // Facet Template A: Primary Triangular Facet

Print 16 copies (or print 1 and use as a reusable cardboard cutting jig) | Base: 280mm, Height: 280mm

How to use this template:
1. Cut along the solid black triangle line with scissors.
2. Place the paper triangle onto your 3mm craft plywood or dense cardboard.
3. Trace the perimeter with a pencil and trace the yellow circles for magnet pocket locations.
4. Make 3–4 light scoring passes with a utility knife along a metal ruler to cut your wood facet.
